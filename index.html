PHASE PLAN

01
FOUNDATION
Weeks 1–8 · Caloric Deficit · Cut 5–7kg
Drop from 20–25% to ~15% body fat
Rep range: 8–12 (higher volume, moderate load)
Sprint intervals: 6–8 rounds × 30 sec
Shorter rest (60–90 sec) to keep heart rate elevated
Focus on form and consistency — not PRs
Calories: 2400–2600 kcal/day (deficit of 300–400)
Protein: 176g/day minimum — non-negotiable
Learn athletic patterns: box jumps, sprints, med ball throws
Target by Week 8
74–76 kg · ab outline visible
02
BUILD
Weeks 9–16 · Maintenance Calories · Recomposition
Build lean muscle while staying lean (body recomp)
Rep range: 4–6 reps (heavier, lower volume per set)
Sprint: 10 × 30 sec or 6 × 40m flat sprints
Add hang power clean — learn weeks 9–10, load weeks 11+
Full 2–3 min rest for compound lifts
Progressive overload: +2.5kg every 2 weeks on main lifts
Calories: 2700–2900 kcal/day (maintenance)
Carb cycle: higher carbs Mon/Fri/Sat, lower Thursday
Target by Week 16
72–75 kg · defined + stronger
03
PEAK
Weeks 17–24 · Slight Deficit · Final Shred
Shred final fat layer to reveal the athletic physique
Rep range: 6–8 (maintain strength, moderate volume)
Sprint: 10–12 × 30 sec or 200m repeats
Optional: 20 min fasted Zone 2 cardio Thursday morning
Week 23 = deload (50% volume, same movements)
Calories: 200–300 kcal deficit from maintenance
Protein: 2.4–2.6g/kg (increase as calories drop)
No eating after 8 PM. Water: minimum 3.5L/day
Target by Week 24
72–74 kg · 10–13% body fat

LIFESTYLE & RECOVERY

Sleep Protocol
8–9 hours per night. Non-negotiable for fat loss and muscle gain.
Fixed schedule: 10:30 PM – 6:30 AM or 11 PM – 7 AM
No phone/screen 30 min before bed — blue light suppresses melatonin
Room dark and cool — 18–20°C is optimal for deep sleep
No heavy meals within 2 hours of sleeping
Magnesium Glycinate 30 min before bed for sleep quality
Daily Foam Rolling
5–10 min daily, ideally before training or before sleep
Quadriceps — 60 sec each side
IT Band (outer thigh) — 60 sec each side
Upper back / thoracic spine — 60–90 sec
Calves — 45 sec each leg
Lats — 45 sec each side
Deload Protocol
Every 8 weeks — a mandatory deload week
Reduce sets by 40–50% of normal volume
Keep the same movements and rep ranges
No new maxes — let your body absorb the training
Feeling weaker during deload is completely normal
You will always come back stronger after deload
Weekly Tracking
Body weight every Sunday morning — fasted, after bathroom
Waist circumference at navel level
Progress photos: front, side, back — same lighting always
Log lifts weekly: squat, deadlift, pull-up, OHP
Rate energy, sleep, recovery on 1–10 scale
Never change multiple variables at once — one at a time
Handling Plateaus
Weight stall 2+ weeks → reduce calories 100–150 kcal/day
Or add 1 extra cardio session per week
Strength stall → check sleep quality, check calorie intake
Overtrained feeling → take 2 rest days, not just 1
Don't panic at the scale — photos and measurements matter more
Stress & Cortisol
High chronic stress elevates cortisol — stores fat around the waist
10 min meditation or breathing daily (Kohli practices this)
Walk in sunlight every morning — lowers cortisol naturally
Limit caffeine after 2 PM to protect sleep quality
Social rest: spend time with people who energize, not drain
06

MINDSET

01
Discipline over motivation
Motivation is an emotion — it fades. Systems and daily habits don't. Build the routine until it's identity.
02
Identity, not sacrifice
Don't say "I can't eat junk". Say "I don't eat junk. I'm an athlete." The language of identity holds.
03
Process over appearance
Train to perform — to run faster, lift heavier, move better. The physique follows performance. Always.
04
Consistency beats perfection
6 months of 80% effort destroys 2 months of 100% effort followed by quitting. Show up every week.
05
Sleep is sacred
Kohli calls sleep his secret weapon. 8+ hours is not lazy — it is how champions are built and fat is lost.
06
No free days from being an athlete
Every workout, every meal, every sleep window counts. The compound effect of small daily choices is everything.
07
Ego is your enemy in the gym
Lifting too heavy with bad form builds injuries, not muscle. Check the ego, master the movement, then load the bar. Athletes train smart first.
08
Comparison is a trap
Your only competition is who you were last week. Comparing your Week 3 to someone's Year 3 is how people quit. Track your own progress religiously.
09
Discomfort is the curriculum
The reps that hurt at the end of a set, the sprint when your lungs burn, the meal-prepped food when everyone else orders out — that's where the transformation lives.
10
Recovery is training
You don't grow in the gym — you grow when you rest. Skipping rest days, foam rolling, and sleep is skipping training. Treat recovery with the same seriousness as lifting.
Final note: At 80kg with 20–25% body fat, you have substantial muscle built over 2 years underneath. The goal is not to build from zero — it's to reveal and refine what's already there, while adding athletic capacity and power on top. Follow this plan for 24 weeks without skipping more than 2 sessions per month and your physique will transform completely.
